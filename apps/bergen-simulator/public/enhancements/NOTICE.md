# R1E public elevation pilot, version 1

Kartverket / Bergen municipality, Bergen 10pkt 2022, last project flight
18 April 2022; publisher Terratec / Field. Native 0.25 m DOM and DTM,
ETRS89 UTM zone 32N (EPSG:25832), NN2000 (EPSG:5941).

Anonymous public height exports follow [Kartverket free-data terms](https://www.kartverket.no/api-og-data/vilkar-for-bruk),
CC BY 4.0; [Høydedata export guidance](https://test.hoydedata.no/LaserInnsyn2/help_no/topics/idh-topic230.htm).
Source catalogue/route distinctions and acquired hashes are recorded in
docs/bergen-simulator/r1e-license-and-access-register.md and r1e-source-inventory.json.

Footprints and original identities: © OpenStreetMap contributors,
[ODbL 1.0](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
The separately distributed catalogue retains original OSM rings and attribution.

Modified by Bergen Reimagined: deterministic surface fitting, constrained roof
triangulation and schematic wall massing. Source-derived estimates, **not a building survey**.
Held-out residuals measure fit to the same acquisition, not independent accuracy.
Roof form/ridge inferred, not surveyed. No measured facade openings, materials,
current appearance or legal feasibility claimed. Colours are artistic.

Six existing buildings with unknown height receive an opt-in render envelope.
No new building is admitted. The original snapshot, terrain, saved scenarios and
R1B/R1C assets are unchanged. Old terrain contact is retained; foundations remain
schematic. Low-power, distant views or failed integrity checks retain originals.

No imagery, private registry information, point-cloud viewer buffers or restricted
municipal meshes are included in this asset.
