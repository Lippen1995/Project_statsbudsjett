## Integrity and transformations

The GeoTIFF, service/project metadata and two original photos have SHA-256 and exact
URLs in the input lock. Checksums detect local/upstream drift, not publisher
signatures. `tests/fixtures/r1b-dom.tif` retains the small raster for offline regression.
The compiler checks the original R1 geography checksum and open-export/datum metadata
before emitting separate assets; it never writes `public/data/`.

Local x/z ↔ WGS84 uses the original origin (5.3235 E, 60.395 N), Earth radius
6371008.8 m, x east / z south, followed by pyproj always_xy to EPSG:25832. Heights
remain absolute NN2000. A 0.8 m inward footprint buffer reduces eave/ground mixing.
Symmetric gable axes are inferred from the longest source footprint edge. Robust
least squares fits ridge/eave envelopes; inlier error ≤0.65 m, fraction ≥85%, pitch
10…65° are screening rules, not independent accuracy certificates. Four northern
gables and Skur 11 pass. Two mixed-roof fits fail and retain original OSM dimensions.

Photo registrations use the coherent northwest-to-southeast facade sequence,
source footprints, unique adjoining white pair and visible gables. They are manual
inferences, not surveyed control points. Trace pixels and crop rules are reproducible
in the compiler. No synthetic windows, dormers, doors, missing storeys or source
connections were inserted. Matte colour, lighting and 18 mm overlay separation are
presentation choices. Source differences/age are visible in the inspector.
