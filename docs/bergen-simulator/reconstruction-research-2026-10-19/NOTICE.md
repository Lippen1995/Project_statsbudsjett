# Research-artifact attribution and limitations

These assets are **isolated method experiments**, not replacements for observed
Bergen, newly verified buildings, a source-snapshot revision or a production release.

`assets/skur-r1c.glb`, `skur-indexed.glb` and `skur-meshopt.glb` retain the Skur
contributions of the existing R1C reconstruction:

- © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0, source `way/393025479`:
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Pinned source coordinates/tags remain
  in the repository's original geographic snapshot.
- © Kartverket, Bergen 10pkt 2022 DOM, CC BY 4.0, unchanged source-fitted roof:
  https://www.kartverket.no/api-og-data/vilkar-for-bruk . Inferred fit, not a survey.
- David40226543, Skur11.jpg, 4 April 2009, CC BY-SA 3.0:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Skur11.jpg .
- Jonatan Andersen, Skur 11a/b/c/d, 21 April 2012, CC BY-SA 3.0 Norway:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Skur_11a.jpg ,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Skur_11b.jpg ,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Skur_11c.jpg ,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Skur_11d.jpg .

Photo-derived Skur contributions retain the attributed CC BY-SA 3.0 NO / compatible
original 3.0 terms described in the existing R1C NOTICE. We extracted one unchanged
landmark body, omitted unrelated neighboring bodies/atlas, indexed exact attributes,
and made a separately labelled quantized/compressed derivative. No new survey,
facade photograph, window, material measurement or modelled hidden side was added.
The unknown architecture and approximate detail dimensions remain approximate.

`assets/skur-osm2world.glb` is generated by OSM2World 0.4.0 from the pinned OSM
coordinates/tags, with no external images/material files. Source attribution and
ODbL database obligations remain. The tool's LGPLv3 software licence is separate
from its input data and produced geometry; no tool binary or source is redistributed.
This output has **generic unsupported windows and default dimensions**. Do not
represent it as a faithful or measured Skur reconstruction. Negative XML node IDs
in the transient carrier file are local transport indices, not observed OSM node IDs.

Screenshots use a uniform diagnostic shader and display centering without changing
metre scale. They are not a material-fidelity or georegistration claim. Skur-derived
screenshots retain the above source credits/share-alike obligations; the diagnostic
UI and camera choices are presentation. No endorsement by any source is implied.
The current application and geographic originals are not overwritten by these files.
